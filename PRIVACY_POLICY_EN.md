# Privacy Policy

**Last updated: August 23, 2026**

This privacy policy applies to the **Crash Tiles** mobile application (the “Application”), created by **H. Durmus** as an ad-supported service.

## 1. Data Collection and Usage

Crash Tiles does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, contacts, or precise location. Game progress, scores, settings, and preferences are stored locally on your device.

## 2. Advertising and Third-Party Access

The Application uses **Google Mobile Ads (AdMob)** to display banner, interstitial, and rewarded advertisements. Google may process device information, advertising identifiers, approximate location, ad interactions, diagnostics, and usage data to serve, measure, and protect advertising services. Personalized advertising depends on your device permissions and Google settings. See the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).

## 3. Children’s Privacy

The Application does not knowingly collect personal information from children. Parents or guardians should review device and advertising settings.

## 4. Data Security

Locally stored game data is protected by the security of your device. No Crash Tiles gameplay account database is maintained by the Service Provider.

## 5. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated from time to time. The latest version will be published in this repository.

## 6. Contact Us

For questions about this policy, contact us through [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/hdurmus/crashtiles/issues).
